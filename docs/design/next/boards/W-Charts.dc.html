the rules
One chart is never wider than 60 cells: past that the line only gets thinner.
Across while each chart can have 30 cells or more; otherwise under, one per row.
Height is what the pane has left after the lines above it: 3 to 8 rows.
Scale cpu and connections from zero; memory from just under its lowest, labelled at both ends, so a drift is seen.
History is what the daemon holds (4 minutes at a second a reading). A chart begins where its history does and ends at now.
No samples yet (an outside process): drawn from its current figure, never an empty box.
Graphs are drawn the same in every terminal that draws braille; a graphics protocol would be a later, optional upgrade.
