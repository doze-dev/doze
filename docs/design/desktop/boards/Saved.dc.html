Why this needs work
Today the daemon keeps 4 minutes of stats and its journal in memory, and both are gone when it stops. A day needs the journal's wake and sleep events, and each service's memory at its last wake, written to ~/.doze/projects/shop, a few kilobytes a day.
The numbers are estimates: a sleeping service uses nothing, so what it would use is what it used the last time it was awake.
